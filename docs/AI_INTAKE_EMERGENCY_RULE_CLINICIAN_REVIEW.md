# AI Intake Emergency Rule Clinician Review

No rule currently claims clinical validation. Reviewer must record: rule code/version, language/dialect, intended symptom concept, severity, positive examples, negated/family/historical/hypothetical near misses, unsafe misses, recommendation, reviewer identity, date, and approval status.

Required sampling: EN, Iraqi Arabic, Sorani CKB, mixed-script, punctuation/diacritics, long input, self-harm, chest pain, breathing difficulty, bleeding, unconsciousness. Approval does not convert detector into diagnosis or emergency-service dispatch.
